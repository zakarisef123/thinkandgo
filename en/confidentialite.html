Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy applies to citizens and legal permanent residents of the European Economic Area and Switzerland.

In this privacy policy, we explain what we do with the data we obtain about you via https://thinkgo.ch. When processing it, we comply with data protection legislation, in particular the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This means, among other things, that:

  • we clearly state the purposes for which we process personal data;
  • we limit our collection to the personal data required for legitimate purposes;
  • we ask for your explicit consent where required;
  • we take appropriate security measures to protect your personal data;
  • we respect your right to access your personal data or to have it corrected or deleted on request.

1. Purposes, data and retention period

This website contains no forms. We receive personal information only when you contact us by phone, post or e-mail: your name, your contact details and the information you choose to share about your project. We use it to reply to you and, where applicable, to carry out a mandate. It is kept for as long as necessary for these purposes and for applicable legal obligations.

We do not share this data with third parties, except with your consent or in the cases set out in section 3.

2. Cookies

This website uses no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, and includes no third-party audience measurement service. Fonts and videos are hosted on our own server.

Only two technical preferences may be stored in your browser’s local storage, on your device only:

  • tg-mail-provider: the e-mail service you chose to write to us (Gmail, Outlook, etc.), if you asked for it to be remembered;
  • tg-cookie-notice: the fact that you have read the cookie information notice.

This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data.

3. Disclosure practices

We disclose personal information if required to do so by law or by court order, in response to a law enforcement agency, to the extent permitted by other legal provisions, or for an investigation relating to public safety.

4. Security

We are committed to the security of personal data. We take appropriate security measures to limit misuse of and unauthorised access to personal data. Only the people who need it have access to your data, access is protected and our security measures are reviewed regularly.

5. Third-party websites

This policy does not apply to third-party websites linked from our website (for example LinkedIn, Gmail or Outlook when you choose to write to us through these services). We recommend that you read their privacy policies.

6. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to make changes to this privacy policy. We recommend that you consult it regularly to stay informed of any changes.

7. Access to and modification of your data

If you have any questions or would like to know what personal data we hold about you, please contact us. You have the following rights:

  • the right to know why your personal data is needed, what will happen to it and how long it will be kept;
  • right of access: to access the personal data we hold about you;
  • right to rectification: to complete, correct, have deleted or block your personal data at any time;
  • if you have given us your consent, the right to withdraw it and to have your personal data deleted;
  • right to data portability to another data controller;
  • right to object to the processing of your data, unless there are legitimate grounds for processing.

Please always state clearly who you are, so that we can be sure not to modify or delete the data of the wrong person.

8. Filing a complaint

If you are not satisfied with the way we process your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority (in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, FDPIC).
